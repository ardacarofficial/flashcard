# What's new in v1.3

Hi! v1.3 is about one thing: making the app **easy to use**. One window to start studying, one job per key, and every way to add cards on one page. Here is all of it in five minutes. The details are in the [user guide](en.md).

## How do I start studying?

The same window opens from everywhere: press **Start studying** on **Home** (or just `Enter`), a deck's **Study** button, or a mode tile on the deck page.

In the window, from the top:

1. **Unfinished**: if you left a session half done, it waits at the top. **Continue** picks up where you stopped.
2. **Mode**: **Cards** (flip, say how well you knew it), **Learn** (multiple choice, then written), **Test** (a practice paper with a score) or **Match** (pairs against the clock).
3. **What to study**: five choices, each showing exactly how many cards it brings: **Today’s cards**, **New cards**, **Studied today**, **Struggled with today**, **All cards**.

An empty choice tells you why: "Nothing is due today", "Today's card limit is reached", "Next card in 5 min"… No more dead ends.

`Enter` starts, `Esc` closes. The app remembers the mode you last used on each deck.

The "cards to study today" number on Home, the number in the window and the cards a session really serves now come from the same calculation. With **All decks**, each deck's own daily limits apply.

## The keys: one key, one job

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| `Space` | Flips the card, front ⇄ back, as often as you like. **Never rates.** |
| `1` `2` | Rate: **Still learning** / **I know it** (`1`–`4` with four buttons). Only after you have seen the back. |
| `Enter` | Presses only the focused button. No hidden rating. |
| `H` | Hint. If the word has an example sentence you see the sentence first, then letters. |
| `Esc` | Pause. The session stays open; **Continue** brings you back to the same spot. |
| `?` | The list of all shortcuts (when you are not typing). |

Holding a key down counts as one press, so no more rating ten cards by accident. Shortcuts stay quiet in text boxes, where `Ctrl+Z` only undoes your typing.

Cards on a short learning step may come a few minutes early. When every remaining card really has to wait, the screen says "Next card in N min": wait, study something else, or finish.

## How do I re-study what I studied or missed today?

Easy: in the study window pick **Studied today** or **Struggled with today**. "Struggled" means cards you rated "Still learning"/"Again" or got wrong today.

These and **All cards** are **practice**: study them as much as you like, the schedule does not change.

Other shortcuts:

- **Study again** at the end of a session opens the window with "Struggled with today" selected.
- **Statistics** → **Overview** → **What am I struggling with?** → **Study these**: the words you missed most recently.

## Where did adding and importing go?

All in one place: **Create** → **Add**. Four ways:

| Way | When |
|---|---|
| **One card** | A word or two. Also opens anywhere with `N`. |
| **Bulk** | You like filling in a grid |
| **Paste** | A list copied from a spreadsheet or a document |
| **From a file** | CSV, Anki, Quizlet, JSON, deck package |

Importing is no longer in Settings; it is here (**From a file**). While you map the columns you see your cards live, and in a list like `word ⇥ meaning ⇥ sentence` the sentence lands safely in the example sentence field. When done: **Undo**, **Open deck** or **Study now**, one click each.

Export, backup and printing: **Settings** → **Data** → **Export & backup**.

## How do I connect OpenRouter?

OpenRouter gives you hundreds of models with one key; some are free. Step by step:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Connections** → **AI** → the **Providers** tab.
   <!-- v1.3: verify after X1/X2 -->
2. Press **Add provider** and pick **OpenRouter** in the gallery.
3. **Paste the key**: the "Get a key here:" link opens [openrouter.ai/keys](https://openrouter.ai/keys); create a key there, copy it and paste it into the box. The key is stored only in your computer's keychain, and the app never shows it again. **Next**.
4. **Pick a model**: the list comes from OpenRouter. Search it or type a model name yourself. **Next**.
5. **Test the connection**: a tiny request goes to the model. See "The connection works."? You're set! **Done**.

If it fails, the app says why in plain words: the key was rejected, no credit left, model not found… Fix that and press **Test connection** again. With more than one provider, **Make default** picks the one to use.

The gallery also has **Claude**, **OpenAI**, **Google Gemini**, **Groq**, **Mistral**, **DeepSeek**, **Ollama** and **LM Studio** running on your own computer, and **Custom (OpenAI-compatible)**. Every request is shown to you before it is sent.

Happy studying!
